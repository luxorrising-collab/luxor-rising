answers), and trust, filtered by where Luxor Rising has **unbeatable
on-the-ground authority** — i.e. where we can genuinely write the best article
online, not just another one. Work top to bottom. One article per run.

Egyptologist. Categories: `temples-tombs`, `planning-timing`, `life-in-luxor`,

## Keyword/intent notes
- Lead titles with the question people type ("is … worth it", "how many days",
  "best time to", "is Luxor safe").
- Every temple/tomb article: include current ticket price (EGP + rough €/$),
  opening hours, and "what's open changes — we check before every guest."
- Safety/desert articles carry the most GEO/trust weight: be calm, specific,
  sourced to government advisories, and clear about the genuine caveats.
- Internal-link discipline: each article links to the flagship or a sibling
  guide, plus /concierge-day, plus one product/experience page.

next run moves on. Keep the list fed — add new topics as search trends shift.
