## Best-practice notes

- Keep all 15 headlines distinct (no repeated phrase) — PMax mixes them freely
  with the long headlines and descriptions, so avoid combinations that read oddly.
- Lead with the dream and the invitation; the price/CTA is on the landing page.
- Pin nothing unless a claim must always show; pinning limits PMax's optimisation.
- Pair this text with strong creative: 1200×628 + 1200×1200 + 600×316 images,
  a square + landscape logo, and a short vertical video (the desert-safari reel
  works) so the asset group can serve across all inventory.
